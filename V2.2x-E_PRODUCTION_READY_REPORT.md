### Algorithm Structure ✅
**Total Score**: 100 points distributed:
- **S1 - Domaine Prioritaire**: 60% (dominant factor)
- **S2 - Niveau d'Expérience**: 20%
- **S3 - Contexte & Rôle**: 10%
- **S4 - Objectif & Intention**: 10%

### Questions → Scoring Map ✅
- **Q1**: Niveau d'expérience → S2
- **Q2**: Domaine principal → S1 (60%)
- **Q3**: Objectif professionnel → S4
- **Q4**: Rôle actuel → S3
- **Q5-Q10**: Ajustements S1/S4, preferences

### Recommendation Logic ✅
1. **#1 Recommendation**: MUST match user's S1 domain (highest score)
2. **#2-#3 Recommendations**: Complementary formations, coherent with S1
3. **#4+ Recommendations**: Additional options
4. **Diagnostic**: Derived from S1-S4 scores, traceable to answers

### UX Requirements ✅
- ✅ User feels understood
- ✅ Recommendations feel logical
- ✅ Top recommendation visually dominant
- ✅ Diagnostic statements traceable to questions
- ✅ No implicit claims of strength/expertise without question
